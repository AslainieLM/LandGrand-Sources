## Application to the four existing objectives

These are proposed design interpretations, not findings about Land Grand learners.

| Objective in Chapter I order | Application within existing scope | Boundary to check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Short Science lessons before each level | Identify the selected concept before exploration begins. | Readability and usefulness to parents still require evaluation. |
| 2. Exploration and reviews after each level | Examine whether collection directs learners toward Science information. Revisit that information in the planned review. | Reaching an item or finishing quickly may be possible without using its description. A review has a separate instructional role. |
| 3. Four landform episodes with selected rock and mineral content | Give each episode and level a clear content focus. | The theory does not verify curriculum competencies, geological associations, or the four-episode arrangement. |
| 4. Illustrations with names and short descriptions | Draw attention to the selected visible features and their explanation. | An illustration does not supply a physical specimen or establish recognition of a real sample. |

The important design question is whether a player can progress by movement and item counts while overlooking Science descriptions. This revision identifies that question. It does not claim the problem has been solved, and it does not add quizzes, classification tasks, scoring rules, timer pauses, or forced reading behavior.
