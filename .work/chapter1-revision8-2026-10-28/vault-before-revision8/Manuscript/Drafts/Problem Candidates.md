# Potential Chapter I Problems

Prepared at the user's request for seven or eight potential problems. This is a selection pool, not eight established findings or an approved replacement for the Word chapter.

Survey coverage is broader than the game audience: parents represent children in Grades 4-6; teachers handle Kindergarten and Grades 3-6, according to the CSV grade fields. Grade 5 remains the intended game audience and the group in the researcher observation. Candidates 1-3 now refer to the overall response groups. Any Grade 5-only analysis must be explicitly labeled as a subgroup.

Candidates 1–3 summarize existing survey evidence. Candidate 4 uses the researcher's reported observation. Candidates 5–8 are hypotheses for checking and must not be presented as confirmed Baluan conditions.

## Four-statement shortlist for review

The user proposed retaining the parent/teacher/content concerns and using a possible shortage of demonstration materials as item 4. They requested shorter, clearer sentences. Proposed wording:

1. Parents reported difficulty explaining printed module lessons to their children.
2. Teachers questioned whether modular learning helps learners understand lessons.
3. Survey responses identified landforms, rocks, and minerals as difficult Science topics.
4. Physical rock and mineral samples may be limited for classroom demonstrations.

Items 1-3 summarize the broader survey groups, not Grade 5-only data. Item 4 is unverified: the survey did not establish a materials shortage. The user clarified that the proposed shortage concerns real rock and mineral examples, understood as physical samples. Its observation/teacher source, extent and date remain unspecified; do not extend this clarification to landform models or claim complete absence. Use definite wording such as "are limited" only after the claim and source are established. Land Grand could offer digital representations and descriptions as a supplement, not a supply of physical samples. The separate Grade 5 interest observation remains available as evidence; it is not erased by this proposed shortlist.
