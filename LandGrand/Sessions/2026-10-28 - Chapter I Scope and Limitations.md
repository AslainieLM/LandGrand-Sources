### Scope and Limitations of the Project

This section describes the coverage and boundaries of the Land Grand project. It explains what the game includes and the limits of its use and the survey findings.

#### Scope

This section outlines the learners, content, game activities, and technical requirements included in Land Grand. The proposed game is for Grade 5 learners at Baluan Elementary School. Development would use Unity and C#, with Piskel and Clip Studio Paint for graphical assets. The application would include the following features:

- The game is single player mode only.
- Four episodes will cover Mountains, Hills, Plains, and Plateaus. Each episode will include selected related rocks and minerals.
- Each episode will have 3 Easy, 5 Normal, and 7 Hard levels. The planned total is 60 levels.
- A short lesson will appear before each level. Collected items will have digital illustrations, names, and short descriptions. A review will follow each completed level.
- The game will use two-dimensional graphics, large touch buttons, background music, and sound effects.
- Players will move, jump, and collect items. Challenges will include obstacles, traps, pitfalls, and time limits.
- Players will progress through difficulty modes and episodes. They can replay levels, continue to available levels, and view episode summaries. A final screen will appear after completing all episodes.
- Players may earn up to three stars per level. Star and completion rules will be defined during development.
- The Main Menu will offer Play, Settings, Tutorial, and Exit. Players will select unlocked episodes through the Landform Map.
- The game will automatically save and load data on the device. Saved data will include the player's name, unlocked episodes, completed levels, stars, and collected items.
- The game will work offline on Android phones and tablets running Android 10 and above. Minimum targets are 2 GB RAM and 300 MB free storage.

#### Limitations

This section explains the limits of the game and how the survey findings should be understood. It identifies the boundaries of the project's learners, content, and offline use.

- The game is intended for Grade 5 learners at Baluan Elementary School. The survey covered several grades. Its findings cannot represent all Grade 5 learners.
- Content is limited to the four selected landforms and selected rocks and minerals. The detailed content and its alignment with the curriculum still need validation.
- The game does not include multiplayer, online accounts, online features, cloud storage, websites, and an administrator panel.
- Saved progress stays on the device used. The game does not provide central teacher monitoring or shared learner records.
- The game is designed to support printed materials and teacher guidance. Stars, collected items, and completed levels record gameplay. They do not measure learning gains.
- Digital illustrations provide additional visual support. They do not provide physical samples or hands-on examination of real rocks and minerals.
- The initial survey records views and expectations about learning support. It does not establish that Land Grand improves knowledge.
